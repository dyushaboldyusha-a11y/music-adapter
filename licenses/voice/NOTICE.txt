OpenBLAS (OPENBLAS.txt), CLAPACK (CLAPACK.txt) and libf2c (F2C.txt).
Sources: https://github.com/alphacep/kaldi

Upstream Windows build instructions:
https://github.com/alphacep/vosk-api/blob/v0.3.45/travis/Dockerfile.win

The Windows binary distribution includes the unmodified MinGW-w64
libwinpthread-1.dll (WINPTHREADS.txt), libgcc_s_seh-1.dll and libstdc++-6.dll.
GCC runtime libraries are covered by GPL-3.0.txt with the additional permission
in GCC-RUNTIME-EXCEPTION.txt. Their inclusion does not change Liberty Wave's
own MIT license. Upstream sources: https://gcc.gnu.org/git.html and
https://github.com/mingw-w64/mingw-w64/tree/master/mingw-w64-libraries/winpthreads

Archive versions, official HTTPS URLs and pinned SHA-256 values are recorded
in the accompanying manifest.json. These components and model files are
included locally; speech audio is not sent to any server.
